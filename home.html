The Problem

Kerala's vacant homes face risks
owners can't see.

Monsoon & Humidity Damage

Kerala's extreme humidity and heavy monsoon seasons cause mold, wood rot, corrosion, and structural deterioration in unoccupied homes — often within a single season.

Security Vulnerabilities

Vacant properties are easy targets for trespassers and theft. Without verified regular visits, unauthorized access can go unnoticed for months.

No Way to Verify Work

Non-resident owners have no way to confirm whether caretakers actually visited or if maintenance was completed properly — they rely entirely on trust.

Our Answer

Full accountability, built into every service visit.

Tender Touch is a secure digital platform designed specifically for non-resident Keralites. It combines geo-verified check-ins, time-stamped photo documentation, digital inspection checklists, and a real-time maintenance dashboard — so you always know your home is in good hands, no matter where you are.
